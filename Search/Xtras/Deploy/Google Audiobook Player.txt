Google Audiobook Player

The Google Audiobook Player is where Google Drive, the cloud storage solution, is deployed to host and play audiobooks. Google Drive offers a free plan with 15 GB of storage, accessible across multiple devices. 

One major advantage of Google Drive is that there is no strict file upload limit, so the number of audiobooks you can store depends entirely on their file sizes.

Steps to Set Up the Google Audiobook Player

1. Install Google Drive: Download and install Google Drive on your desired device—desktop, laptop, or mobile.

2. Upload Audiobooks: Add your audiobook files to Google Drive.

3. Organize Your Files: Many audiobooks consist of multiple audio files. It is recommended to create a separate folder for each audiobook to keep everything organized.

4. Choose a Player: On mobile devices, CloudBooks is avaialble on Android while on PC's an Audiobook Player can be found in AppBarn, WebUp or Webology search engine.

5. Enjoy Your Audiobooks: Once set up, you can stream your audiobooks directly from the mobile app or web app anytime, anywhere.
